# Kitchen-test matrix

All recipes remain `Adapted` or `Concept testing required`. Nothing is promoted to `Tested` from static review alone.

Record the cooker model, nominal capacity, program, actual elapsed time, liquid used, texture, flavor, center temperature, changes made, and a photograph for every run.

| Family | Representative | What must be confirmed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yeast rice-cooker loaf | 301 No-Knead Soft Yeast Loaf | Dough height, first-cycle time, safe inversion, center 190–200°F, crumb after cooling |
| Rice-cooker quick bread | 310 Banana Quick Bread | Batter height, gummy-center risk, extension time, center 200–205°F |
| Steamed buns | 320 Mantou Steamed Buns | Basket clearance, water retention, lid handling, fluffy center |
| Egg breakfast | 100 Savory Spinach Egg Rice | Egg addition timing, 160°F endpoint, grain texture |
| Oat porridge | 104 Apple Cinnamon Steel-Cut Oats | Foam level, half-fill limit, second-cycle need, final viscosity |
| Rice and beans | 107 Mexican Smoked Black Bean Bowl | Water-line behavior with canned beans, grain separation, salt/acid balance |
| Quinoa | 115 Indian Masala Quinoa Bowl | Rinse, 1¼-cup starting liquid, model-specific grain setting |
| Lentil dal | 118 Classic Red Lentil Dal | Red-lentil tenderness, liquid reduction, late tadka and acid |
| Brown-lentil bowl | 121 Brown Lentil & Carrot Bowl | Brown-lentil doneness, second cycle, vegetable texture |
| Rice-cooker soup | 134 Burrito Beef & Black Bean Soup | Required broth volume, half-fill limit, pasta/rice texture, beef 160°F |
| Shrimp finish | 127 Peanut Lime Shrimp & Veggie Rice | Late-add timing, opaque center, shrimp 145°F |
| Conventional yeast bread | 3 Basic White Bread | Hydration, proofing, loaf pan, center temperature |
| High-protein bake | 239 Protein Egg Muffins | No protein powder, pan release, egg 160°F, reheating |
| Cold dip | 222 Mediterranean Feta Dip | Blend texture, salt/acid, chill stability, two-hour limit |
| Frozen dessert | 227 Dark Choco Cherry Bark | Yogurt thickness, freeze time, fruit ice crystals, snap |
| Marinade | 240 Quick Protein Marinades | Per-pound quantities, raw-protein handling, glaze burn risk |

Promotion rule: only the tested recipe and its directly equivalent variation may move to `Tested`; related recipes stay untested until the formula change is documented and justified.
