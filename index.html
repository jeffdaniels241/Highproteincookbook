Substitution Matrix & Troubleshooting

Cottage Cheese vs. Yogurt

Use blended Cottage Cheese for high-heat applications like warm dips and baked pizza dishes. Use Greek Yogurt for acidic leavening in bagels or cold preparations.

Whey vs. Casein

Whey Isolate is best for post-cook additions. Casein acts as a structural stabilizer, absorbing significant liquid to create thick puddings and moisture-rich bakes.

Rice Cooker Bread Troubleshooting

Center is gummy or wet

Causes: Undercooked, sliced too soon, or batter too wet.

Fix: Run extra cook time in 10–20 min chunks. Cool longer before slicing. Reduce liquid 2-4 tbsp next time.

Loaf is dense/heavy (Yeast breads)

Causes: Underproofed, yeast is old, or dough was too dry.

Fix: Let the final rise in the pot get visibly puffy. Add water 1 tbsp at a time until dough feels tacky next time.

Top is pale or soft

Causes: Normal for rice cooker moisture environment.

Fix: Invert the loaf back into the pot and cook 5–15 minutes, or finish under an oven broiler.

Bread sticks to pot

Causes: Insufficient greasing or high sugar content.

Fix: Grease more thoroughly, use a parchment round at the bottom, and rest 10 minutes before turning out.
