Cottage Cheese vs. Yogurt

Use blended Cottage Cheese for high-heat applications like warm dips and baked pizza dishes. Use Greek Yogurt for acidic leavening in bagels or cold preparations to maintain probiotic integrity.

Whey vs. Casein

Whey Isolate is best for post-cook additions as it tends to dry out under sustained heat. Casein acts as a structural stabilizer, absorbing significant liquid to create thick puddings and moisture-rich bakes.
